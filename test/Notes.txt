These tests were made over the course of development.  They may 
or may not be functional. They may also test features that may
not have been fully implemented. Run them at your own risk. The
worst that will happen is that it will contaminate your DB. To 
run, move to the root folder of the app and execute.  Some of 
these tests employ pytest and need to be executed with
appropriate arguments.

Think of them as starting points for your own tests.
